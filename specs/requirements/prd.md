# Hello World Webapp

## Problem Statement

The team needs a minimal working example of a web app that talks to an API, to prove the platform's sign-in, web app and service pieces fit together end to end.

## Solution

A small web app where a signed-in user sees a hello message that the app fetches from a backing API.

## Actors

- Signed-in user: anyone who signs in through the organization's SSO; can view the greeting.

## Features

- F1 [Greeting page](features/F1-greeting-page.md)
- F2 [Greeting API](features/F2-greeting-api.md)

## Product-wide

See [Product-wide](product-wide.md).

## Out of Scope

- Editing or storing custom greetings.
- Personalised greetings by name.
- Admin or role-specific experiences.
